# v1.7.2

**Fixes the Vref card disappearing after a Vref or range command.**

## Fix

- Right after a Vref, calibration switch, range or Rfb command the page prefers that command's reply; when the reply carried no Vref or range data (a refused command, a value above the ceiling) the whole card went with it. It now falls back to the device status, so the card and its controls stay.

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v1.22.0**.
