# v1.10.0

**NH Studio / NH Pocket sign in with an account automatically; the Relays page no longer manages app tokens. Card buttons no longer stick out of the card.**

## Sign-in

- The "Signed-in apps (app tokens)" section is gone from the Relays page. NH Studio / NH Pocket remember the account they signed in with and sign in again every time they start, so there are no tokens to revoke or manage by hand.
- NH Studio / NH Pocket cards on the Relays page show "Signed in: account" or "Not signed in". NH Studio headless and NH Hub need no sign-in and show neither.
- When the same app signs in again, the server ends its previous token, and a token unused for 30 days ends by itself, so tokens no longer pile up.

## Fixes

- When a Relays card is narrow, the Manage and Delete buttons wrap to the next line instead of sticking out of the card.

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v1.23.0**.
