# v1.13.1

**The 2D heatmap names the pin behind every row and column; the GPIO45 leak compensation switch now applies as soon as it is flipped.**

## Visualization

- The 2D heatmap labels each row with its analog pin (A0…) on the left and each column with its select pin (D0…) on top, as printed on the board; row/column mirroring flips the labels too. The P numbers in the cell corners still match the CSV columns. Nothing is shown until the device reports its pin layout.

## Reference voltage (TIA-CTL v2.0.F)

- The "compensate GPIO45 pull-down leakage" switch was only a draft that reached the device with "Apply"; the next refresh showed the device's real value again, so it seemed to turn itself back on. It now applies at once like the other switches and changes only that setting. "Apply" sends just the reference Rfb, and the two reading switches no longer send the leak setting along.
- Leak compensation does not need conversion or smart Vref; it works in manual mode too. It only needs the row's Rfb measured and D13 (GPIO45) among the scanned columns.
