# v1.7.1

**Fixes the Vref and range panel: Virtual and Auto could not be chosen after measuring Rfb, and a checkbox was misaligned.**

## Fixes

- "Measure Rfb" and the Vref sweep were cut off by the backend's 2 s command wait: `rfb_measure` takes about 1.5 s and times out when the network is a little slow, and a sweep always did. `rfb_measure` now waits 15 s and a sweep waits as long as its steps, settle and capture times need (up to 120 s).
- The panel now remembers what the device answered: once Rfb is measured, Virtual and Auto can be chosen at once, without waiting for the next status update.
- The "Compensate the GPIO45 pull-down leak" checkbox uses the same style as the other settings and lines up with its text.

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v1.22.0**.
