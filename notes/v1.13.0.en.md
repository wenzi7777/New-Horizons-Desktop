# v1.13.0

**Vref is now manual plus two switches, and recordings note how a v2.0.F read its rows; the FindMe card no longer flips between two sets of values.**

## Vref (TIA-CTL v2.0.F)

- The Raw / Virtual Rfb / Auto buttons are two switches: "Convert to the reference scale" (virtual Rfb) and, on top of it, "Smart Vref" (auto). Both off is manual (raw), read the way v1.5.F and earlier boards read. Converting needs a measured Rfb, and Smart Vref needs the conversion on so the readings' scale stays put. The firmware's three modes are unchanged.
- Manual keeps the Vref slider and safety bands, and says "same scale as v1.5.F and earlier boards" when Vref is 330 mV. With Smart Vref on, the locked slider is replaced by the current Vref and how many times it was adjusted since boot.
- The reference Rfb and GPIO45 leak compensation moved to "Advanced", next to Rfb measurement, the calibration switch, the ceiling and the Vref sweep.

## Recording

- `.meta.json` has a new `range` block: `mode`, `vref_mv`, `ref_rfb_ohm`, `leak_compensation`, and per-row `rfb_ohm` and `offset_mv`. Only a v2.0.F has one; other boards write `null`. A change of mode, Vref, reference Rfb or leak compensation during a recording is logged in `changes`, so you can tell later which part compares directly with older boards.

## FindMe

- The FindMe card in device settings no longer flips between two sets of values (Gateway host alternating between the device's own IP and the relay's, last success between a time and a number). The relay's presence snapshot wrote the device's IP and a time into the fields the device uses for the relay's address and its uptime in ms; the relay's values now go to `device_addr` and `last_findme_at`.
- "Last success" reads "N s after boot".

## Device settings

- On/off settings (IMU, stream buffer, custom manifest, update at boot, log, filter, raw ADC, leak compensation and the external LED mode) use the same switch as the apps.
- The IO visualizer has the v2.0.F board picture.
- Apps: a running card shows the external LED strip next to the OLED and status LED (apps with `drive_ext_led`); it says "shared" when more than one app may light it, and that the strip stays dark when the external LED mode is not App. Button icons are centered.
