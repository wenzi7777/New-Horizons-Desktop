# v0.6.1

## VD-CTL/R v1.5.F battery and LED support

- Adds a **Detect battery ID** control in Device Settings. It asks the device
  to resample the POGO ID resistor and then refreshes the reported profile.
- Keeps charging current manual at a safe 100 mA by default when no valid ID
  is present. Known 1k and 10k POGO IDs are reported as automatic profiles.
- Shows v1.5.F's nine external WS2812B LEDs on GPIO16 instead of the v1.0.F
  three-LED configuration.

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v0.17.0**.
