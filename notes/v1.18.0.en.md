# v1.18.0

**The magnetometer gets an on/off switch (off by default) and a hard/soft-iron calibration wizard, with reminders when the calibration is due or looks off. The IMU card shows the chip's actual ODR.**

## Magnetometer switch

- A magnetometer block next to the IMU on the device settings' Streaming tab: an On / Off menu with Apply, and the calibration reminder interval (7 / 14 / 30 / 90 days or never).
- Choosing On and applying goes straight into the calibration wizard. Leaving it without applying a calibration switches the magnetometer off again.
- The block shows the chip's ODR, averaging and measured rate. On GCU boards with the IMU off, it says the magnetometer is blocked by the IMU.

## Calibration wizard

- **Prepare**: fit the case and battery, and keep away from laptops, steel desks and motors.
- **Rotate**: an animation shows the moves (figure eight, then each face). A rotatable 3D sphere split into 26 cells lights up the directions already covered and marks the next one to turn towards, with a coverage ring and point count underneath.
- **Result**: before/after XY / XZ / YZ projections, the field strength (about 47 µT around Aizu), the residual (above 5% it suggests redoing) and the mode. With at least 70% coverage and 300 points it fits a full ellipsoid; with less it calibrates hard iron only.
- The new fit is layered on the calibration in use, and cancelling keeps the old one.
- The Maintenance tab has a magnetometer calibration entry too, for redoing or clearing it at any time.

## Due and drift reminders

- While the magnetometer is on, Launchpad device cards show a badge when the calibration is due within 3 days, overdue, suspected off, or missing. The device settings' Maintenance tab gets a dot, and the magnetometer block shows a banner with Calibrate now.
- The firmware raises "suspected off" when the calibrated field strength stays more than 20% off for 5 minutes. A nearby magnet triggers it too, so it only reminds and never disables the magnetometer.

## Other

- The IMU card shows the chip's ODR (with new firmware it follows the scan rate).
- Terminal gains `set-mag` / `set-mag-calibration` command builders; the manual's Devices chapter covers the magnetometer.
