# v1.7.0

**The Vref and range panel for the TIA-CTL v2.0.F: set Vref, the calibration switch, measure Rfb, the range mode and the Vref sweep.**

## Vref and the calibration switch

- The Vref card on the device settings page gains: a Vref input, back to 330 mV, the ceiling, and a button for the calibration switch.
- Above about 800 mV a reboot can fail to boot, and the page says so; Vref returns to 330 mV on every reboot.

## Rfb

- A "Measure Rfb" button: each row's Rfb, ADC offset and status (OK, no Rfb soldered, too large for this Vref, nothing on the calibration network). The Vref to measure at can be given separately.

## Range

- Range mode: Raw (as read), Virtual Rfb (re-expressed as the reference Rfb would have read it at 330 mV, so a calibration made on a 20k board still applies) and Auto (virtual, and lowers Vref while rows hit the rail). The reference Rfb and the GPIO45 leak compensation can be set.
- Virtual and Auto need Rfb to have been measured; the card shows how many rows hit the rail in the last frame.

## Vref sweep

- Step Vref at a steady pressure; the table lists Rfb, the median Rs and the cells at the rail for each Vref, and shows how much Rs moves across the sweep (a pure resistance should be near 0). The full CSV can be downloaded.
