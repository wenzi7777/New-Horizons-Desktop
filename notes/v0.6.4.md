# v0.6.4

## Reliable battery time estimates

- A v1.5.F status refresh now keeps the MAX17048 rate reported by the full
  status response when later v5 stream packets update only voltage and state
  of charge.
- Overview therefore retains its remaining-time or time-to-full estimate
  between regular telemetry packets instead of reverting to unavailable.

## Stable device identity

- Gateway-relayed status results now retain the device name nested in their
  complete status data, preventing a brief fallback to the bare device UID
  after Refresh status.

Companion firmware: **New Horizons OS v0.17.5** (no firmware change).
