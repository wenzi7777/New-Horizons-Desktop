# v1.17.0

**The Visualization card gains Transpose (swap the X and Y axes); Gyro / Acc are drawn as graphics, and the magnetometer is shown.**

## Transpose

- A Transpose checkbox next to Row Mirror / Col Mirror swaps rows and columns (6 × 8 shows as 8 × 6); the size label, 2D heatmap, 3D surface and COP follow.
- Transpose first, then mirror: Row / Col Mirror always flip what you see top-bottom / left-right, and the three checkboxes give all 8 orientations (Transpose + Col Mirror = a quarter turn clockwise).
- Display only: recordings and CSVs are unchanged; P numbers stay with their sensors and the A.. / D.. pin labels swap with the axes. The choice is remembered in this browser.
- Transpose is unavailable while a sensor-layout profile is selected (the profile places its sensors itself).

## IMU and magnetometer

- Gyro / Acc in the stats row become three panels, each a gauge + the last 10 s of X / Y / Z as trend lines (red / green / blue, auto scale) + the values in the same colours:
  - Accelerometer: a spirit level (the bubble moves to the high side), |a| and roll / pitch.
  - Gyroscope: centred X / Y / Z bars, scale switching between 30 and 2000 °/s.
  - Magnetometer (new): a dial with the heading of the board's +X (tilt-compensated with the accelerometer), µT values and |B|. The heading is raw, not a precise compass. Devices without a magnetometer say so.
