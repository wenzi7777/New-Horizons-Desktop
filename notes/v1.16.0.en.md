# v1.16.0

**A new "Illustrated" interface: the device overview is a drawing of the board, with live values tied to the actual parts by leader lines. Classic stays the default; switch in the top bar.**

## Illustrated interface (v2.0.F / v1.5.F / v1.0.F)

- A new "Interface" menu (Classic / Illustrated) next to the language menu in the top bar, remembered in this browser. Classic is the default and looks as before.
- Device settings → Overview: a vector drawing of the board (positions from the PCB files, following the light or dark theme) with clickable values on both sides — battery, reading mode, scan FPS and matrix, connection, firmware, lights. A click opens the matching settings section. Scan and pins are one "Matrix" label whose leader branches to both FPCs.
- Power and connection paths: USB-C / battery → charging → system, and device → relay → server, with the path in use drawn solid.
- IO Visualizer: a close-up of both FPCs, one square per pin, lit when enabled.
- Reading mode, scan preset and update channel use large choice buttons (a check on the selected one); nothing is sent until you press Apply, as before.
- GCU LTS boards have no drawing and keep the existing cards.

## Other

- Fixed the v1.5.F / v2.0.F digital FPC pin labels: contact 18 = GND, 17 = SCL (GPIO 42), 16 = SDA (GPIO 40), 15 = NC (they were shifted).
- While charging, the battery icon draws its bolt inside the battery with a cut-out outline, so it stands out.
- The IO Visualizer board picture is capped at 460 px; the Vref panel shows where Rfb0–13 sit on the back of a v2.0.F and which are soldered (from rfb_measure).

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v2.3.1**.
