# v0.6.3

## Battery at a glance

- Device Overview now presents a compact battery card with an appropriate
  battery or charging icon, current percentage and a rate-based estimate of
  remaining runtime or time to full charge.
- The estimate is withheld when the device has no battery or no usable live
  rate; it is an estimate from current MAX17048 telemetry, not a health score.

## Low-battery warning control

- On v1.5.F Hardware Settings, the onboard battery LED's low-battery warning
  threshold can be saved from 0 (disabled) to 25 percent.
- Existing battery profile, manual charging-current and automatic ID detection
  controls are unchanged. The new control is hidden for unsupported boards.

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v0.17.5**.
