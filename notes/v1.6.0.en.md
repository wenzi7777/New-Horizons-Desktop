# v1.6.0

**Recognises the new TIA-CTL v2.0.F board, shows a battery identified by its ID resistor, and adds a Vref card.**

## TIA-CTL v2.0.F

- Adds the v2.0.F board: the same matrix and pins as v1.5.F (14 × 14), firmware updates on the `arduino-v20f` track, with a wiki page.
- The SDK's virtual devices gain the v2.0.F board.

## Battery

- When the firmware identifies the battery by its ID resistor, the battery card says so and shows the capacity and charge current, and hides the manual form; the details gain a battery ID row.
- With no ID resistor (a JST battery) or an unknown value, the manual setting applies as before.

## Vref

- The device settings page gains a read-only Vref card: the current value, the ceiling, the power-on value (the DAC's EEPROM) and the calibration switch.

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v1.21.0**.
