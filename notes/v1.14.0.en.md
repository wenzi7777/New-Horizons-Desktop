# v1.14.0

**The TIA-CTL v2.0.F reference voltage panel now offers three reading modes (ADC mV, conductance in µS, conductance with smart Vref), and calibration works in conductance too; recordings also keep each frame's unit, Vref and saturated rows.**

## Reading modes (TIA-CTL v2.0.F)

- The reading is one select on the reference voltage panel: ADC mV (the default), conductance (µS), or conductance with smart Vref, sent with Apply. "Scale to reference" and the reference Rfb are gone.
- Conductance needs Rfb measured first. The Rfb table shows each row's status (ok, not fitted, saturated, open, ADC failed), and notes that unmeasured rows read 0.
- Leak compensation is a select with Apply.

## Recording

- When the device sends per-frame range information, the CSV gains three columns: `range_unit`, `vref_mv` and `saturated_rows`.
- The `.meta.json` range block now records the reading mode, output unit, whether smart Vref is on, Vref, Rfb, offsets and each row's status; the calibration block records the profile's unit (`unit`). The recording summary shows the reading mode, and older firmware's virtual / auto shows as "legacy virtual".

## Calibration

- The calibration workbench shows the profile's unit. When it differs from the current output unit it says the profile is kept but not applied, and how to switch back or calibrate again; in conductance it notes that the calibration stays valid when Vref changes.
- Sensors on rows without an Rfb are not counted as fit failures; progress counts only sensors with an Rfb and says how many were left out.
- New error messages: the output unit changed during calibration, and the profile is in the other unit.
