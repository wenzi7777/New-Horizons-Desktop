# v0.6.2

## Battery status at a glance

- Device cards now show the v5 battery percentage directly beside charging
  state, with tabular numerals for stable live updates.
- Battery packets received on the Arduino stream are merged into the device
  status cache, so the percentage does not wait for a manual status poll.

## Built-in LED control

- Hardware Settings adds a separate brightness control for the board's system
  and battery WS2812B LEDs.
- The default is 30%; external FPC LED settings remain independent.

Companion firmware release: **New Horizons OS v0.17.3**.
