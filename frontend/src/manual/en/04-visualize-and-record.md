# Live view and recording

## Adding a device to the view

1. Open **Visualize**.
2. Click **Add Device View** and choose a device. A card with its live data appears.

## Reading the card

- **2D Heatmap** and **3D Surface** switch the view.
- **Range** sets the values that map to the lowest and highest colors. **Reset** goes back to the default.
- **Show COP** (at the top) marks the center of pressure.
- **Row Mirror** and **Col Mirror** flip the matrix if it looks upside down or mirrored.
- Pick a profile on the card to draw the sensors on a picture of the insole or glove. Profiles are made in **Profile**.

## Recording

- Press **Record** on a card to start, and **Stop** to end.
- To start several devices at the same time, tick the checkbox on their cards and use **Rec Selected** / **Stop Selected** at the top.
- After Stop, the button may show **Finishing…** for a moment while the last data is written. The recording still ends at the moment you pressed Stop.
- If the card says the server is behind, nothing is lost. Wait for Finishing to end before closing the page.

Every recording goes to **CSV Export**, in a folder for the device and the date.
