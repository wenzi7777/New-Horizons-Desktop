# Getting started

NH Console shows the live data from your pressure-sensing devices, records it, and lets you set the devices up.

## Signing in

Sign in with the account your administrator gave you. There are two kinds of account:

| Account | What you can use |
| --- | --- |
| admin | Everything: Home, Visualize, Relays, Profile, CSV Export, Apps, Studio, Tools |
| user | Visualize and Profile |

Lab accounts are shared, so the app never uses the account name as your name.

## The top bar

- **Left**: click **Console** and the version number to see the version, the lab and the release notes for every version.
- **Middle**: the pages. Which ones you see depends on your account.
- **Right**: the sun/moon button switches between light and dark. **Auto** (half sun, half moon) follows sunrise and sunset in Aizu; click to step to Light, then Dark, then back to Auto. Your choice is remembered in this browser. The book icon opens this manual. Next to it are the language menu (English, 日本語, 简体中文), your account and **Logout**.

## Where to start

1. Connect your devices through a Gateway or a Hub. See **Connecting devices**.
2. Check that they show up on **Home**.
3. Open **Visualize** to see the live data and record it.
4. Download or analyze the recordings in **CSV Export** and **Tools**.
